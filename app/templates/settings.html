Reading time

{% call tip.help("How the page time limit is applied") %} Comics use a limit per page visit. EPUBs and web novels use a separate limit per 300 words, with at least 30 seconds per text passage. Hidden readers pause timing. A passage reaching its limit does not contribute to words-per-minute estimates. Changing a limit is not retroactive — every page already recorded keeps the limit it was measured under, so your statistics never change behind you. {% endcall %}
